Shopify packaging EPR apps: 9 apps, 3 reviews, and no leader yet

A cited teardown of the packaging EPR reporting category on the Shopify App Store: prices, ratings, coverage, and where the gaps are.

Data collected Oct 18, 2026 (PT). Listing details came from the App Store pages as seen in cached listing pages, not direct page loads. Anything marked "estimate" is our judgment.

The short version

A Shopify App Store search for "epr packaging" returns about nine apps built for packaging EPR reporting. Eight of the nine show zero reviews. The only rated one, EPR Insights, has 5.0 stars from 3 reviews. So the category is crowded with products but has almost no social proof. Nobody has won it yet.

That matters for anyone building here. When nine apps all promise the same workflow and none has reviews, the merchant has very little to choose on. Price, clarity, and trust signals end up doing most of the work.

What merchants are being asked to pay

Prices cluster low. Here is what the listings show:

  • Ceendesis: $9, $39, or $99 a month, metered at 200, 1,000, and 10,000 orders.
  • Packlog: a free plan, then $9.99 or $14.99 a month, metered by products mapped.
  • EUReady: one plan at $12 a month, bundling GPSR product-safety fixes with LUCID and Citeo reports.
  • Tarra: $14, $59, or $119 a month, with the widest coverage claim (9 countries).
  • EPR Insights: $15, $29, or $59 a month, plus $0.05 per order over each plan's limit.
  • PackRegistry: $19 a month for the first 50 stores ("Founding DE"), then $39. Germany only.
  • Packaging EPR: $29 for one country or $49 for all, with no free trial shown.
  • PacForm: free setup, then $48 a year. US state EPR only.
  • EPR Pack Report: $179 a year for one country (plus $49 a year per extra country) or $249 a year for all.

Entry and mid tiers sit between $9 and $59 a month. The top tiers reach $99 (Ceendesis) and $119 (Tarra). Only two of the nine lead with yearly pricing.

One small trust problem: PacForm's card in search results showed "$10/month", while its listing's pricing section showed free plus $48 a year. A merchant comparing cards would see a different price from the one they'd actually pay.

Everyone built the same product

Read the listings side by side and the feature set converges. You build a packaging library, map packaging to products (by tag, type, vendor, SKU match, or AI suggestion), aggregate orders by country and material, then export registry files such as LUCID XML along with an "evidence pack" for auditors.

The differences are at the edges:

  • Depth in one country. PackRegistry does only Germany, with LUCID report types and BOM versioning.
  • Breadth. Tarra claims 9 countries. EPR Insights adds batteries, electronics, and single-use plastics.
  • Multi-channel. Only Ceendesis advertises CSV import for Amazon, eBay, and wholesale orders.
  • Liability posture. Tarra says you review and file the reports yourself and that it never submits. EPR Pack Report says it can file your reports automatically. Those are very different promises to make to a merchant worried about fines.

Where the gaps are

  1. Reviews. With 8 of 9 apps at zero, the first app to collect a couple of dozen genuine reviews should gain an edge in search and conversion (estimate).
  2. Done-for-you setup. Every listing still asks the merchant to weigh and map their packaging. Only EPR Insights mentions "EPR experts" and a custom plan. A paid setup tier looks unclaimed.
  3. Sellers who aren't only on Shopify. One app supports Amazon and eBay imports. Merchants selling across channels are underserved.
  4. Deadlines. Only Packlog advertises deadline reminders. A per-country filing calendar is cheap to build and easy to explain.
  5. Pricing that matches the filing cycle. Filing is periodic, so annual pricing probably fits how merchants budget better than monthly does (estimate).

What we'd do

If you're thinking of entering: don't ship another generic multi-country exporter at about $15 a month. Nine apps already do that. Pick a wedge (one country done deeply, multi-channel sellers, or done-for-you setup) and lead with it.

If you're already in the category: spend the next 60 days on reviews and onboarding, not on adding countries (estimate). Ask for a review right after the first successful export, since that's the moment the merchant sees value. Make sure your search-card price matches your listing.

Sources

All listing data was seen on the Shopify App Store on Oct 18, 2026 (PT): the search page loaded directly, and the nine listings as cached listing pages.
