Real samples

Executive summaries from three reports. Full reports cite every figure. Tap a title to open it.

Category Teardown · data collected Oct 18, 2026EU/US packaging-EPR reporting apps on the Shopify App Store
  • The category is crowded but has no leader. A Shopify App Store search for "epr packaging" returns about 9 apps built specifically for packaging-EPR reporting. They sit alongside unrelated ERP and shipping-protection apps.
  • Almost nobody has social proof yet. 8 of the 9 dedicated apps whose listings we reviewed show 0 reviews. The only rated one is EPR Insights at 5.0★ from 3 reviews.
  • Prices cluster at $9–$59/month for entry and mid tiers. Top tiers reach $99–$119/month. Two apps charge per year ($48/yr, $179–249/yr). Two have a usable free plan.
  • Products are converging on one design: map packaging to products, then aggregate orders by country and material, then export registry files (LUCID XML, CITEO/SYDEREP, UK, US state) plus an "evidence pack".
  • What decides the winner is unclear, which is the opening. Nobody yet competes on reviews, done-for-you setup, or depth in one country. Coverage claims range from 1 country to 9.

Bottom line: do not enter as "another multi-country EPR exporter at ~$15/mo." If you're already in the category, spend the next 60 days on reviews and onboarding, not on adding countries (Estimate, reasoning in the Recommendations).

Snapshot format · data collected Oct 19, 2026WooCommerce PDF invoice plugins on WordPress.org
  • Huge, but with one dominant plugin. A WordPress.org search for "woocommerce pdf invoice" returns 625 plugins. The leader, PDF Invoices & Packing Slips (WP Overnight), has 300,000+ active installs, six times the runner-up's 50,000+ (WebToffee).
  • The leader is also the best rated: 5.0★ from 1,865 ratings, and only 0.75% of them are 1–2★ (14 ratings). Displacing it on quality is unrealistic (Est.).
  • Upgrades are sold as yearly site licenses: WP Overnight Pro is €69 / €139 / €279 for 1 / 3 / 25 sites. WebToffee Pro is $69 / $99 / $199 per year for 1 / 5 / 25 sites.
  • Satisfaction varies widely among mid-sized plugins. Their share of 1–2★ ratings ranges from 3.5% to 16% (table).
  • Niche wedges already exist. Country add-ons for Italy (4,000+ installs) and Japan (300+, updated Oct 19, 2026) show that buyers pay for local requirements.

Bottom line: do not launch another general invoice plugin. Win on a narrow need, such as a country's invoice requirements or an add-on for the leader (Est.).

Snapshot format · data collected Oct 19, 2026B2B and wholesale pricing apps on the Shopify App Store
  • Very crowded. The App Store's B2B category label shows 1,905 apps. A search for "wholesale pricing" returns 13+ apps with "B2B Wholesale Pricing" in or near their name.
  • Ratings no longer separate anyone. The leaders sit at 4.9–5.0★: BSS (1,191 reviews), Sami (993), Wholesale Pricing Discount B2B (747), Clay (286), Massy (225).
  • Pricing has converged: free, then about $25–29/mo, then about $45–50/mo (where net terms usually unlock), then $99/mo (API and ERP sync).
  • Free plans are the norm: 11 of the 13 named B2B apps show "Free plan available".

Bottom line: do NOT enter with another all-in-one B2B pricing app. The only openings are bundling features that rivals sell in higher tiers or separate apps, and being clearer about limits (Est.).
